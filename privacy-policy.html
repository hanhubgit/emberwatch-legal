Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes how Emberwatch Uploader ("the app"), the private publishing tool of the Emberwatch Music YouTube channel, handles data.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the owner of the Emberwatch Music channel to publish the channel's own content. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • An OAuth access/refresh token for the channel owner's own Google account, used solely to upload videos and thumbnails and to set titles, descriptions, tags and publish times on the owner's own channel (scope youtube.upload).
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and title) to confirm the upload goes to the correct channel (scope youtube.readonly).

The app does not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

How the data is used

The data above is used only to publish the channel's own videos. It is not used for analytics, profiling or advertising.

How data is stored and shared

The OAuth token is stored only in a local file on the channel owner's own computer. It is not stored on any server operated by the app, and it is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app does not share any API data with anyone.

Cookies and device data

The app is a command-line tool, not a website or web app. It does not place, access or recognise cookies or similar technologies on any device or browser, and it does not collect device information. These pages do not use cookies or analytics.

Data retention and deletion

  • The OAuth token is kept only while the owner uses the app and is refreshed automatically by Google.
  • Channel information is read at upload time and is not stored.
  • The owner can delete the stored token at any time by deleting the local token file. It is also deleted within 7 days after access is revoked or the app is no longer used.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the app from uploading.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests about your data: open an issue at github.com/hanhubgit/emberwatch-legal/issues or contact us through the Emberwatch Music channel page.
