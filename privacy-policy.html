Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes how Emberwatch Uploader ("the app"), the private publishing tool of the Emberwatch Music YouTube channel, handles data.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the owner of the Emberwatch Music channel to publish the channel's own content. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • An OAuth access/refresh token for the channel owner's own Google account, used solely to upload videos and thumbnails and to set titles, descriptions, tags and publish times on the owner's own channel.
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and title) to confirm the upload goes to the correct channel.

The app does not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

How data is stored and shared

The OAuth token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer or private server. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from uploading; locally stored tokens are then deleted.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new date.

Contact
