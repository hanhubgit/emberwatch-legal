Emberwatch Music

Emberwatch Music is a YouTube channel publishing long-form, instrumental medieval and fantasy music for focus, study, reading and relaxation.

Channel: youtube.com/@EmberwatchMusic

Emberwatch Uploader is the channel's private publishing tool. It is used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions to the channel through the YouTube Data API. It does not offer accounts or services to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
